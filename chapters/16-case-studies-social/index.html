Remember for the interview

  1. 01Chat: one WebSocket per device on gateways that only hold connections; a Redis session registry maps user and device to gateway, and the chat service owns message logic.
  2. 02Order messages by a per-conversation sequence number assigned by the single owner of that conversation, and dedupe retries with a client-generated message ID. Never order by client clocks.
  3. 03Ack the sender only after a durable quorum write. Delivery is at-least-once with client dedupe by seq, and receipts are per-member cursors (last_delivered_seq, last_read_seq), not per-message rows.
  4. 04Presence is heartbeats plus a TTL key plus subscribe-on-view; push notifications are a wake-up hint, and offline devices catch up by syncing from the server-side log.
  5. 05Feed: fan out post IDs, not bodies. Push to followers for normal authors, pull celebrity posts at read time and merge, skip inactive users, and treat the home timeline as a rebuildable cache.
  6. 06Ranked feeds are candidate generation plus scoring at read time; paginate with an opaque cursor over a per-session ranked list, and use keyset cursors on time-sortable IDs for chronological feeds.
  7. 07Notifications: accept with 202 and an idempotency key, apply preferences, quiet hours, caps and templates once, then use separate queues and workers per channel and priority so OTPs never wait behind campaigns.
  8. 08Exactly-once delivery to APNs, FCM, SMS or email providers is impossible; aim for at-least-once with dedupe keys and collapse IDs, retry only transient errors, and disable dead tokens.
  9. 09Video calls have three planes: control (REST), signaling (your own WebSocket service carrying SDP and ICE candidates) and media (SRTP over UDP). ICE tries host, STUN server-reflexive and TURN relay candidates; TURN over TLS on 443 rescues UDP-blocked networks.
  10. 10An SFU with simulcast is the default for group calls: each sender uploads once in several qualities and the SFU picks a layer per receiver. Mesh suits 1:1, an MCU suits dial-in and composited output, and SFUs cascade across regions.
