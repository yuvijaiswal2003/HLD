Remember for the interview

  1. 01Metrics tell you that something is wrong, traces tell you where, logs tell you why; link all three with a trace or correlation ID propagated on every hop.
  2. 02Use structured JSON logs, RED metrics for services and USE metrics for resources; keep high-cardinality fields like user IDs out of metric labels.
  3. 03Measure latency with percentiles from histograms, never averages, and never average percentiles across instances; merge histogram buckets instead.
  4. 04SLI is the measurement, SLO the internal target, SLA the contract with penalties; a 99.9% SLO leaves about 43.8 minutes a month of error budget.
  5. 05Spend the error budget on releases and experiments; when it runs out, the error budget policy shifts the team to reliability work.
  6. 06Page on symptoms users feel, using multi-window burn-rate alerts (for example 14.4x over 1 hour and 5 minutes), not on CPU or other causes.
  7. 07Sessions are instantly revocable but need a lookup; JWTs verify locally but cannot be revoked before expiry, so pair short-lived access tokens with revocable refresh tokens and a denylist.
  8. 08Use OAuth 2.0 authorization code with PKCE for every user-facing client, OIDC ID tokens for login and SSO, and check iss, aud and exp on every token.
  9. 09RBAC for simple roles, ABAC for contextual policies, ReBAC (Zanzibar-style tuples) for sharing and nested resources; enforce coarse checks at the gateway and fine-grained checks in services.
  10. 10TLS outside and mTLS inside, envelope encryption with KMS at rest, secrets in a vault with workload identity, passwords hashed with Argon2id or bcrypt, and layered DDoS defense at the edge.
