How to use this course

  1. Read the lessons in orderEach chapter builds on the ones before it. The diagrams and tables carry as much as the text.
  2. Take the chapter quizAim for 70% or more. Every answer explains why it is right, and why the tempting option is wrong.
  3. Practice a design out loudRun the 45-minute timer, sketch your answer in the notes, then open hints one at a time before the model solution.
  4. Revise across chaptersMixed practice shuffles questions from any chapter and re-drills the ones you missed. The cheat sheet collects every key takeaway.

The 45-minute interview

Most HLD rounds follow this shape. Chapter 1 explains what to say in each phase, and every design problem has a timer that walks you through it.
